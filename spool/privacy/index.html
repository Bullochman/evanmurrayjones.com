Spool — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Spool is a personal location-history archive. It is built local-first and privacy-first. This policy describes exactly what Spool does and does not do with your data. It is written to match the app's actual behavior — if the two ever disagree, that is a bug; please report it.

The short version

  • Your location history is stored on your device. By default it never leaves your phone or iPad.
  • We (the developer) cannot see your data. There is no Spool account, no Spool server that receives your history, and no analytics that include any location.
  • Sync is opt-in and end-to-end encrypted. If you turn on iCloud Sync, your archive is stored in your own private iCloud (CloudKit private database) under your Apple ID. Location-bearing fields are written to CloudKit's encrypted values, so Apple cannot read them and neither can we.
  • No third-party trackers or advertising SDKs. None. This is reflected in the app's privacy manifest and "Data Not Collected" nutrition label.

Network access

Spool makes no network requests to any Spool-operated server — there is no such server for your data.

The app does contact Apple's own services in these narrow, standard cases:

  1. Map tiles are loaded from Apple Maps (MapKit) to draw the map, the same as any Maps-based app.
  2. Place names are resolved on-device. Turning a coordinate into a human-readable place name uses a reverse geocoder bundled inside the app (GeoNames data), so your coordinates are not sent anywhere to be named.
  3. Address search (only when you ask for it). If you type an address while adding or naming a place, that text query is sent to Apple's geocoding service to find the matching location. This happens only when you actively search an address — never in the background.

If you enable iCloud Sync, the app uses Apple's CloudKit to store your archive in your private iCloud database and to deliver change notifications across your devices. This is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy.

Sub-processors

  • Apple Inc. — Maps and address search (as described above), and CloudKit/iCloud (only if you enable sync).

When optional server-side AI for complex questions ships (a future, opt-in, per-query feature), the provider (Anthropic) will be disclosed here as a sub-processor, the feature will be clearly marked as "this leaves your device," and the endpoint will be zero-data-retention. It is not part of the current app.

Your controls

  • Delete any range of history, the last N days, or everything — permanently, with no soft-delete or server copy, from Settings → Data.
  • Export your full archive (GPX / CSV / GeoJSON) at any time from Settings → Data.
  • Turn sync off at any time; your local archive remains intact.
  • Lock the app with Face ID / passcode.

Children

Spool is not directed at children and collects no personal information for any user.
