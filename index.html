Alabama Autism

Everything autism in Alabama — cited, current, and free.

Public-good web app· AI + cited knowledge base· Free, no login
Visit alabamaautism.info →

A trusted, always-cited resource for parents of children with autism in Alabama — an AI chatbot grounded in a two-scope knowledge base with scope-badged citations, a 300+ provider directory, and a suite of free parent tools (just-diagnosed roadmap, ready-to-send school/IEP letters, SSI & Medicaid estimators). It refuses to give medical advice, cites every answer, and says so when it doesn't know.

  • Cited AI answers — Alabama-specific, with source badges and verify dates
  • 300+ provider directory — filter by region, service, age, Medicaid
  • Free parent tools — roadmap, letters, deadlines, SSI/Medicaid estimators
  • Self-updating — nightly crawler keeps figures and policy current
  • Built for the tired ones — the just-diagnosed and the years-in
